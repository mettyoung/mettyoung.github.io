**Effective 27 September 2026**

This privacy policy explains how the Android app **Deconstruct Chinese** (package `com.mettyoung.deconstructchinese`, "the app") handles your information. The app is developed by Emmett Young (Mettyoung), based in Singapore ("we", "us").

## The short version

- There is no account or login.
- There are no ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
- Your translation history, Vocab Watchlist and word counts stay on your device.
- Text you submit for translation is sent to a third-party AI service (Alibaba Cloud's Qwen) to produce the translation. We do not store it on our own servers.
- We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

## What the app does

Deconstruct Chinese helps you learn Chinese. You type text to translate, and the app breaks the result down into its vocabulary. You can "watch" words; the app keeps a count of how often each watched word comes up and shows them in a Vocab Watchlist ranked by that count.

## Information stored on your device

The following is stored **only locally on your device**:

- your translation history;
- your Vocab Watchlist; and
- the frequency counts for watched words.

This data is not uploaded to us. Because it exists only on your device, it will be lost if you uninstall the app or change phones. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Text you submit for translation

When you ask the app to translate text, that text is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection directly to a third-party AI model service, **Alibaba Cloud's Qwen API (Model Studio / DashScope)**, solely to generate the translation and vocabulary breakdown shown to you.

We do not store this text on our own servers. Alibaba Cloud processes it under its own terms and privacy policy: [Alibaba Cloud International Website Privacy Policy](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/legal/latest/alibaba-cloud-international-website-privacy-policy).

Please do not submit sensitive personal information (for example, passwords, ID numbers, financial or health details) for translation.

## In-app purchases

We plan to offer optional in-app purchases (such as a subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock) through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never receive your card or payment details. Google may share with us the purchase status and order information needed to give you access to what you bought. Google's handling of your information is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What we don't do

- We don't require an account or collect your name, email or contact details through the app.
- We don't show ads or use advertising or analytics SDKs.
- We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

## Children

The app is not directed at children; its target audience is adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help address it.

## Security

Text sent for translation is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security, so we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when new features such as in-app purchases launch. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
